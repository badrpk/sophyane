How to play

🏓

Control your paddle

Use W/S or ↑/↓ on a keyboard. Mouse and touch users can move directly on the court.

⚡

Read the bounce

Hit the ball near a paddle edge to change its angle and pressure the CPU.

🏆

Reach seven first

The first side to score seven points wins. Start a new match anytime.

Match tips

Stay near center.
Recover after each return so you can cover both high and low shots.
Use paddle placement.
The farther from center you hit the ball, the sharper its return angle becomes.
